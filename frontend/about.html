Our Story

Who We Are

Edens Refrigeration and Air-Conditioning Limited is a Christchurch-based company providing professional heating, cooling and refrigeration services to homeowners and businesses across the region. We work on everything from a single household heat pump to full commercial refrigeration and cool room systems.

Our team is made up of experienced, fully trained technicians who take pride in doing the job right the first time — whether it's a new installation, a routine service, or an urgent repair. We believe in straightforward communication, fast appointments, and honest, quotation-based pricing with no hidden surprises.

Every customer, big or small, residential or commercial, gets the same level of care and attention. That's the standard we've built our name on.
